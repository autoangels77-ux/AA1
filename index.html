FAQs

Does my vehicle need to be insured?
Yes. Vehicles handed over to Auto Angels should have a valid comprehensive insurance cover.
Who will drive my vehicle?
Customer vehicles are handled by licensed Auto Angels team members who prioritise safety and professionalism.
Can I choose my own workshop?
Yes. Customers may choose their preferred workshop within Colombo and Greater Colombo areas.
How are payments handled?
Workshop payments are settled directly by the customer. Auto Angels fees may be paid via online transfer or cash.
What happens if repairs take multiple days?
Revisit charges may apply depending on travel distance. WhatsApp repair updates are provided free of charge.

Please Note

Auto Angels currently does not undertake spare-part sourcing or purchasing services.

Customers are requested to ensure sufficient fuel is available and personal valuables are removed prior to vehicle handover.

How Auto Angels Works

Simple, transparent and convenient from start to finish.

1. Vehicle Handover

Your vehicle is handed over at your home, office or another convenient location.

2. Initial Inspection

A quick inspection is carried out and repair requirements are discussed.

3. Workshop Coordination

Your vehicle is taken to your preferred workshop or an Auto Angels partner workshop.

4. Repair Updates

Customers are updated on repair costs and timelines before work proceeds.

5. Vehicle Return

Once repairs are completed, the vehicle is returned safely back to the customer.
