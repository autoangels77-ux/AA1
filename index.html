FAQs

Does my vehicle need to be insured?
Yes. Vehicles handed over to Auto Angels should have a valid comprehensive insurance cover.
Who will drive my vehicle?
Customer vehicles are handled by licensed Auto Angels team members who prioritise safety and professionalism.
Can I choose my own workshop?
Yes. Customers may choose their preferred workshop within Colombo and Greater Colombo areas.
How are payments and service fees handled?
Workshop payments are settled directly by the customer. Auto Angels collection and return charges are benchmarked against prevailing ride-hailing rates based on distance and location, while a separate concierge fee is charged depending on the coordination and time involved in managing the repair or service process.
